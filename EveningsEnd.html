EVENING'S END

SYNOPSIS

Evening’s End follows a group of Thieves* on a picnic that ends in an unexpected disaster. This squad of Thieves consists of four individuals, Kento, Rin, Erabu and Velenia, who have grown to call each other family through the obstacles that brought them together. They finally get a breather and plan a quiet, small and wholesome picnic to spend some time together. However, fate has other plans for them and disaster strikes in their moment of peace and quiet.

After a round of archery, Erabu goes to collect the arrows and is faced with a poisoned snake that goes mad and bites her. The poison seeps into Erabu, alters her DNA and triggers her to transform into a large snake and lose her sanity. This accident creates a shift in the squad and they scramble to help Erabu come back to herself and in the process things don’t go as they intended for it to go. As the saying goes, disaster strikes when you least expect it. Will this immobilize the Thieves? Will Erabu survive the evening? Will this be the end for them? *Thieves is a placeholder squad name. The squad name hasn’t been decided yet.

Characters

Rin

Rin comes from an admirable samurai/knight clan. She is gifted at wielding a sword and throwing knives.

Kento

Kento is a young boy with the ability to transform into a wolf. He is timid at first encounter but slowly grows comfortable. He is an expert thief and is known for his infamous bread heist.

Erabu

Erabu is a young girl with snake ancestry that allows her to transform into a snake. However, the transformation causes her to shift personalities and become an alternate version of herself where her typical self is kind and quiet becomes angry and mean. She tends to be the sweet angel to the squad and everyone adores her when she is her typical self.

Velenia

Velenia is the princess of the royal family. She was born with the ability to manipulate elemental magic. Due to the endless boredom and politics that her family was involved in, she decides she needs fresh air and leaves the palace to practice her magic in the real world. She is the life of the squad or even a party if there was ever one.
